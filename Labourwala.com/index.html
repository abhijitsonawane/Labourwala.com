Every Worker’s Right
Labour Laws Simplified

01

Minimum Wages Act, 1948

Har worker ko state-notified minimum wage se kam pay nahi hoga. Labourswala pe register karte hi aapka wage automatically state schedule ke according set hota hai. Maharashtra min. wage: ₹14,842/month (unskilled).

Enforced on Platform
02

Contract Labour (R&A) Act, 1970

Contract workers ko regular employees jaisi basic facilities milni chahiye — canteen, rest rooms, first aid. Labourswala ke sab registered contractors iss act ke under licensed hain. Aapki safety hamari priority hai.

All Contractors Licensed
03

Payment of Wages Act, 1936

Wages time pe milni chahiye — har mahine 7-10 tarikh tak. Platform pe escrow payment system hai jo guarantee karta hai ki koi contractor aapka paisa rok nahi sakta. Digital payment, full transparency.

Escrow Payment System
04

Building & Construction Workers Act, 1996

Construction workers ke liye special protections — safety gear mandatory, accident insurance, welfare board registration. Labourswala pe sare construction workers ka welfare board registration assist kiya jaata hai.

Safety Verified
05

Employees' Provident Fund Act, 1952

20+ employees wale establishments mein EPF mandatory hai. Workers ki salary ka 12% employer contribute karta hai. Retirement ke liye savings — Labourswala ke platform pe EPF compliance automatically track hoti hai.

EPF Compliance Tracked
06

Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948

Medical benefits, maternity benefits, disability benefits — ESIC registration workers ki basic right hai. Platform pe eligible workers ka ESIC registration automatically process hota hai. Bimari mein paisa nahi rukta.

ESIC Registered
07

Inter-State Migrant Workers Act, 1979

Doosre state se aane wale workers ko extra protections milti hain — travel allowance, displacement allowance, aur home-state wages. Labourswala migrant workers ke rights monitor karta hai real-time mein.

Migrant Rights Protected
08

Sexual Harassment at Workplace Act, 2013

Workplace pe harassment ke against zero tolerance. Labourswala pe ek secure complaint mechanism hai — anonymous reporting, 24hr response time. Sabke liye safe work environment hamari non-negotiable commitment hai.

Zero Tolerance Policy
